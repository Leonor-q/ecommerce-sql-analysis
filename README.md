# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a fictional e-commerce company's sales data using SQL.

The goal is to demonstrate how SQL can be used to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights.

The analysis focuses on:

* Sales revenue
* Product performance
* Category performance
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Monthly sales trends
* Average order value
* Business insights based on the available data

The dataset is synthetic and was created specifically for this portfolio project.

---

## Business Questions

The project answers the following business questions:

1. How many orders have we received?
2. What is the total sales revenue?
3. Which product generated the most revenue?
4. Which product category generated the most revenue?
5. Which customers have spent more than €200?
6. How many orders has each customer placed?
7. Which customers have placed at least 3 orders?
8. Who are the highest-spending customers?
9. What is the average order value (AOV)?
10. Which products have sold the most units?
11. Which products have generated the least revenue?
12. How many units have we sold in each category?
13. What is the monthly revenue?
14. Which month generated the most revenue?
15. What percentage of total revenue comes from each category?

## Database Schema

The database consists of four related tables:

```text
customers
    │
    │ customer_id
    ↓
orders
    │
    │ order_id
    ↓
order_items
    │
    │ product_id
    ↓
products
```

### Tables

**customers**

Contains customer information such as name, email and country.

**products**

Contains product information including product name, category and price.

**orders**

Contains individual customer orders and order dates.

**order_items**

Contains the products and quantities included in each order.

## Key Business Insights

### Total Revenue

The dataset generated total revenue of **€5,618.36**.

Revenue represents sales generated by the business and should not be confused with profit.

---

### Top Revenue Product

**Headphones** generated the highest revenue among all products, with **€959.88**.

However, the product with the highest number of units sold was **Notebook**, with 17 units.

This demonstrates that sales volume and revenue are different metrics.

```text
Units sold = quantity of products sold

Revenue = quantity × price
```

A product can therefore sell a large number of units without generating the highest revenue.

---

### Revenue by Category

The Electronics category generated **€3,669.33**, representing approximately **65.3% of total revenue**.

The remaining revenue was generated by:

* Office: €772.63
* Accessories: €639.68
* Home: €536.72

Electronics is therefore the main revenue-generating category in this dataset.

> Note: Revenue should not be interpreted as profit because the dataset does not contain product costs or operating expenses.

---

### Average Order Value

The average order value (AOV) was approximately **€112.37**.

AOV represents the average amount of revenue generated per order.

---

### Monthly Revenue

September generated the highest monthly revenue at **€1,183.71**.

Further analysis showed that approximately **77.7% of September's revenue came from the Electronics category**.

This suggests that the September revenue spike was strongly associated with Electronics sales.

> Note: The dataset does not contain information about promotions, marketing campaigns, seasonality, or external events. Therefore, the underlying cause of the September increase cannot be confirmed.

## Business Insights

### 1. Monthly Revenue

September generated the highest monthly revenue at **€1,183.71**, significantly above the other months in the dataset.

Further analysis showed that approximately **77.7% of September's revenue came from the Electronics category**.

This suggests that the September revenue spike was strongly associated with Electronics sales.

> Note: The dataset does not contain information about promotions, marketing campaigns, seasonality, or external events. Therefore, the underlying cause of the September increase cannot be confirmed.

### 2. Revenue by Category

Electronics generated **€3,669.33**, representing approximately **65.3% of total revenue**.

This makes Electronics the main revenue-generating category in the dataset.

From a business perspective, this category could be an important area for further analysis, particularly regarding product performance, inventory management, and customer demand.

> Note: Revenue should not be interpreted as profit because the dataset does not contain product costs or operating expenses.

### 3. Revenue by Product

**Headphones** generated the highest revenue among all products, with **€959.88**.

However, the product with the highest number of units sold was **Notebook**, with 17 units.

This demonstrates an important distinction between sales volume and revenue:

* **Units sold** measures how many products were purchased.
* **Revenue** depends on both quantity and price.

Therefore, a product with a high sales volume does not necessarily generate the highest revenue.

### Overall Business Insight

The analysis shows that revenue is concentrated in the Electronics category, with Headphones being the highest-revenue product.

September recorded an unusually high revenue level, primarily driven by Electronics sales. Further investigation would be required to determine the underlying reasons for this increase.
